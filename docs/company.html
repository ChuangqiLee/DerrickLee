02 / RESEARCH CAPABILITIES

An integrated scientific platform.

Five experimental platforms, complemented by research planning and scientific communication support.

01

BIOINFORMATICS

Bioinformatics & multi-omics

From public datasets to single-cell and spatial insights.

  • Clinical & population databases: UKB, GBD, CHARLS and NHANES.
  • Single-cell sequencing and analysis; spatial transcriptomics, metabolomics and proteomics.
  • Mendelian randomization and research-oriented data analysis.
02

CELL & MOLECULAR

Cell & molecular assays

Connect cellular behavior with molecular evidence.

  • Cell culture, transfection, stable cell lines, viral packaging, and cell sorting.
  • WB, ELISA, qPCR, Co-IP, ChIP, flow cytometry, immunofluorescence and microscopy.
  • Proliferation, apoptosis, cell cycle, invasion and migration; drug-resistant cell line screening.
03

MECHANISM RESEARCH

Mechanisms & organoids

Investigate the biological processes behind a research question.

  • Protein modifications including lactylation, palmitoylation, phosphorylation and acetylation.
  • Programmed cell death, glycolysis, senescence and phase separation.
  • Organoids and organ-on-chip; tumor immune microenvironment and drug-response research.
04

PRECLINICAL STUDIES

Animal models & imaging

Link disease models, tissue evaluation and imaging.

  • Animal care, breeding, disease models, dosing and tissue sampling.
  • Oncology and cardiovascular models, PDX, clinical sample testing, IHC, IF and H&E staining.
  • Small-animal MRI, micro-CT and ultrasound/photoacoustic imaging; ethics and project records.
05

BIOMATERIALS

Biomaterials & drug delivery

From functional materials to biological evaluation.

  • Design and characterization of stimulus-responsive and molecular-recognition materials.
  • Nanoparticles, nanozymes, hydrogels and bacterial delivery; brain, lymphatic and tumor targeting.
  • Controlled drug release; efficacy and safety studies at molecular, cellular, tissue and animal levels.
06

RESEARCH SUPPORT

Research design & communication

Support clear study plans and responsible scientific communication.

  • Research question refinement, study design and technical consultation.
  • Grant proposal consultation and scientific writing guidance.
  • Manuscript editing, language polishing, journal selection and submission-process guidance.

  1. 01

    Consult & assess

    Discuss the research question, feasibility, budget and timeline.

  2. 02

    Technical discussion

    Clarify experimental details, answer questions and arrange lab visits as needed.

  3. 03

    Agree on the plan

    Refine the proposed work and confirm the scope of collaboration.

  4. 04

    Set milestones

    Confirm the final protocol and project reporting milestones.

  5. 05

    Execute & report

    Carry out the work with periodic progress and results discussions.

  6. 06

    Deliver & review

    Deliver analyses, figures, experimental reports, records and similarity-check reports.
