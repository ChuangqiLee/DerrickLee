01 / LLM agents · Scientific writing

PaperOrchestrator

Journal of King Saud University Computer and Information Sciences

Accepted 2026.03

Read paper ↗Publication record ↗

Research question

Investigate how an organized LLM pipeline can turn researcher-supplied methods, experimental results, references, and figures into a structured manuscript, while reducing truncation and formatting failures.

System & approach

The system uses seven fixed-order stages: outlining, section-by-section drafting, length control, full-text polishing, BibTeX generation, robust LaTeX conversion, and journal-template rendering. A shared PaperContext transfers information between stages; a Continue Mechanism, Length Guard, and section-level conversion support completeness and formatting robustness.

Evaluation & findings

The paper evaluates 60 samples across NLP, computer vision, and biomedical AI against 11 baselines. It reports 96.7% section completeness, a 90.0% LaTeX compilation rate, and an overall human-evaluation score of 3.85/5. Ablations assess the contributions of the continuation and conversion modules.

Scope & limitations

These are writing-quality and structural metrics, not proof of scientific correctness. The paper identifies hallucinated references and unsupported results as unresolved risks; generated manuscripts require researchers to verify sources, data, and claims.

02 / Human–AI interaction · Education

A multimodal teaching agent for K–12 learning

International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction

Accepted 2026.01

Read paper ↗Publication record ↗

Research question

Explore how a multimodal AI teaching agent supports mathematics learning across school stages and urban–rural contexts, and identify when the same interaction design helps or hinders learners.

System & approach

The architecture integrates fine-tuned LLAMA-13B content generation, personalized speech synthesis and voice cloning, and an enhanced Wav2Lip pipeline with SyncNet and dynamic time warping for audiovisual alignment. Interactive question answering, automatically generated slides, and configurable narration and pacing form the teaching workflow.

Evaluation & findings

A three-week mathematics study involves 300 students from Grades 5, 8, and 11, balanced across urban and rural settings. The paper compares the agent with search-and-read self-learning using achievement tests, usability and immersion ratings, interaction logs, and learner interviews. Primary and junior-high cohorts show learning gains and modest reductions in urban–rural gaps.

Scope & limitations

Grade 11 learners performed worse with the agent. The paper links this to insufficient explanation depth, cognitive overload, and trust concerns, highlighting the need for stage-specific content, adjustable pacing, and transparent reasoning.

03 / Human–drone interaction · Edge AI

Skybound Magic: body-only drone piloting

International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction

Accepted 2025.08

Read paper ↗Publication record ↗

Research question

Design a controller-free human–drone interface that translates whole-body gestures into flight commands using lightweight, local vision processing and understandable feedback.

System & approach

YOLOv8-Nano locates the operator, a MobileNet-based PoseNet extracts 17 skeletal keypoints, and a rule-based classifier maps eight poses to flight commands. The Jetson Nano implementation combines skeletal overlays, LED signals, and synthesized voice with error recovery and emergency-stop prioritization.

Evaluation & findings

The study tests 168 participants in indoor and outdoor environments, collecting 20,160 gesture interactions. It reports 91.8% recognition accuracy, 101 ms average response latency, and a mean usability rating of 4.6/5. Questionnaires and qualitative observations examine learnability, feedback, and professional use contexts.

Scope & limitations

Performance varies with the environment and user characteristics. The current vocabulary is limited to eight poses; continuous gestures, more inclusive interaction design, and broader environmental adaptation remain future work.
